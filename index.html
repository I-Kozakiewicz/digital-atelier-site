What I need from your business

Send what you have. If something is missing, we’ll identify it together before I quote.

  • Your business name, what you offer, who you serve and what you want visitors to do.
  • Your logo, brand colours, existing website or social profiles, any copy you have, and examples of sites you like.
  • The pages and languages you need, including whether you have translated copy.
  • Your public contact details, opening hours, social links and preferred enquiry or booking process.
  • Your target launch date and whether you already have a domain or hosting.

Photos for your website

I’ll ask for photos of your business, products, team or premises, and for permission to use them. If you don’t have suitable photos, I can source licensed stock images or generate illustrations for you to approve. Stock and generated visuals can illustrate your services, while photos of your actual team, premises or products should come from you.

ABOUT DIGITAL ATELIER

Work directly with the person building your website.

I’m a freelance web designer and developer based in Algiers. I hold a university degree in Information Technology, Computing and Design from the United Kingdom and have experience creating websites for businesses.

I work directly with founders and small teams, including businesses launching their first website. I explain the choices, share a working preview and refine it with you before handover.

How a project works

  1. Understand the business

    We discuss your audience, services, languages and the most important actions visitors should be able to take.

  2. Design and review

    You review a working preview. Together we refine the content, layout and contact routes.

  3. Test and prepare for launch

    I check the site on desktop and mobile and verify navigation, language switching and contact links before handover.
