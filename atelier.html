Selected projects

Three concept projects exploring privacy, shared space and how buildings meet the landscape.

Residential concept

The Courtyard House

A shaded courtyard and generous openings organise a home around light and privacy.

View project

Design approach

Daily living opens onto a planted courtyard. Recessed windows and deep thresholds provide shade and soften the transition between private rooms and shared spaces.

Public interior concept

The Reading Room

An intimate public interior with flexible seating and a quiet material palette.

View project

Design approach

A shared table anchors the room, with quieter seats along the edges. Timber shelving and soft daylight give the interior a consistent character without making every area feel the same.

Residential concept

The Olive Grove Retreat

A compact dwelling responds to the slope and preserves the trees that frame it.

View project

Design approach

The building follows the slope so the terrace can sit among the existing trees. Local-toned stone and timber screens bring the building into the palette and rhythms of the site.
